# FEC Playbook™: Pre-Built System & Support Content Recommendation

## Recommended Strategic Shift

The site should make one distinction impossible to miss: **FEC Playbook™ is not a blank software platform that an operator must learn, configure, and fill with their own automation logic.** It is a pre-developed revenue operating system built from FEC experience, then adapted to the facility’s brand, team, and existing stack.

The current homepage already communicates *what* FEC Playbook™ helps an operator do: manage inquiries, follow-up, reputation, repeat visits, and accountability. The next content layer should communicate *why implementation is different*: the operational systems are already built. The customer brings their venue knowledge and brand; FEC Playbook™ supplies the ready-to-adapt playbook and the implementation support.

> **Core positioning statement:** You are not buying another piece of software to build inside. You are adopting a pre-built FEC revenue system, shaped around the work your team already needs to do.

## The Message Architecture

| Message layer | What the visitor should understand | Recommended language |
|---|---|---|
| **Category** | This is an operating system, not a generic CRM. | **FEC Revenue Operations, ready to run.** |
| **Mechanism** | The automations and sequences are pre-developed. | **The playbook is already built.** |
| **Personalization** | The venue does not get a generic, one-size-fits-all deployment. | **You bring the brand. We make the playbook yours.** |
| **Confidence** | Help is present through activation, not just at login. | **FEC-specific implementation support from a team that understands the work.** |
| **Conversion promise** | The Revenue Review is a concrete working session—not a generic product tour. | **See which ready-built playbooks fit your facility first.** |

The approved offer hierarchy should remain visible whenever this message appears: **8 Revenue Playbooks, 13 Core Modules, and 100+ pre-built automations.** These are tangible proof points for the “already built” claim, not generic feature counts.

## Highest-Impact Homepage Changes

### 1. Strengthen the Hero Subheadline

The revised hero headline is working and should remain. Replace only the body copy beneath it with the version below.

> **FEC Playbook™ gives your team a pre-built system for party and group inquiries, guest follow-up, reputation, repeat visits, and accountability. The automations and sequences are already built from FEC operating experience—then adapted to your brand, team, and existing software.**

Keep the primary CTA as **Book a 30-Minute FEC Revenue Review**. Replace the helper line below it with:

> **See the ready-built playbooks that fit your facility—and the revenue work they take off your team’s plate.**

This is more effective than “do not replace your POS” as the first reassurance because it answers the buyer’s more immediate concern: *Will we have to build and manage another system ourselves?* The existing **“No rip-and-replace required”** line should remain as the lower-risk technical reassurance.

### 2. Replace the Hero Proof Strip With an Adoption Proof Strip

The current strip lists operational categories. Move that language into the Revenue Cycle section and use the hero strip to resolve the three largest adoption anxieties: build effort, brand fit, and help.

| Card | Headline | Supporting line |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **PRE-BUILT FOR FECs** | Eight revenue playbooks and 100+ automations start ready—not as a blank canvas. |
| 02 | **MADE YOURS** | Your voice, offers, handoffs, and team structure shape the rollout. |
| 03 | **SUPPORTED THROUGH ACTIVATION** | FEC-specific guidance helps your team put the right playbooks into motion. |

Use the existing black background, cyan rules, and sharp left-to-right geometry. Each card should be labelled with a large two-digit number and a small play-button triangle motif—not rounded SaaS cards.

### 3. Add a “The Playbook Arrives Built” Section Before Platform Proof

Place this section directly after **The FEC Revenue Cycle** and before the product screenshots. This is the best point in the narrative because visitors have just seen the revenue moments; now they can understand that the system for each moment is already constructed.

**Eyebrow:** `PRE-BUILT BY FEC EXPERIENCE`  
**Heading:** `THE REVENUE SYSTEM IS BUILT. YOUR BRAND MAKES IT YOURS.`  
**Body:** `Most software gives your team a set of tools and a blank screen. FEC Playbook™ starts with the revenue workflows, automations, and handoffs already mapped for Family Entertainment Centers. We then tailor the system to the way your facility communicates, sells, and serves families.`

Use three stacked, diagonal-edged blocks rather than a generic centered card grid.

| Block | Copy | Suggested visual cue |
|---|---|---|
| **01 — START WITH A REAL PLAYBOOK** | `Party inquiries, booking confirmations, review requests, repeat-visit follow-up, team accountability, and more begin with systems already designed for FEC operations.` | Large `08` watermark; cyan play-button pointer. |
| **02 — ADAPT IT TO YOUR VENUE** | `We align the message, offer, timing, owners, and handoffs with your brand and the systems your team already uses.` | “YOUR BRAND” in cyan with an angular field. |
| **03 — PUT IT INTO MOTION WITH SUPPORT** | `Your team gets FEC-specific implementation guidance so the playbooks become part of daily operations—not another login that goes unused.` | An operator-to-team handoff diagram, not a stock support icon. |

**Section CTA:** `See What Your Revenue Review Covers →` linking to `/book-a-demo`.

### 4. Reframe the Existing Revenue Playbooks Section

Keep the eight-card layout but update its lead copy so it reads as pre-developed operating systems rather than a library of features.

**Current direction:** “Purpose-Built Systems for the Moments That Move Revenue.”  
**Recommended heading:** `EIGHT READY-BUILT PLAYBOOKS. ONE CONNECTED REVENUE SYSTEM.`

**Recommended body:**

> `These are not starter templates for your team to build from scratch. Each playbook is a pre-developed FEC system with the workflows, automations, ownership, and follow-up structure needed for a specific revenue moment. We tailor the details to your venue.`

The footer beneath the cards should read:

> `You bring the brand. We provide the playbook—and the support to put it to work.`

### 5. Make the Support Promise Concrete on the Demo Page

The demo page needs the same action-led visual treatment as the homepage. Its current copy describes the review well but does not show why the customer will be able to activate what they see.

Change the fourth “We’ll Cover” item to:

> **The support path that gets your playbooks live**  
> `Understand the implementation work, team participation, and support available from kickoff through activation.`

Add a fourth expectation card after the calendar or immediately above it.

**Heading:** `YOU WILL NOT BE HANDED A LOGIN AND LEFT TO BUILD.`  
**Body:** `Your Revenue Review identifies the ready-built systems that matter first. From there, we tailor the playbooks to your facility and guide your team through activation.`

The page should not say **“unparalleled support”** on its own. That is an absolute claim that creates skepticism unless it is substantiated with a specific operating commitment. Say what makes support distinct instead.

## Support Language: Use Proof, Not an Unqualified Superlative

“Unparalleled support” is directionally valuable, but it is strongest when it is translated into a buyer-verifiable promise. Until response-time, onboarding, implementation, or account-management commitments are documented, use the following approved-style language.

| Use case | Recommended copy |
|---|---|
| Homepage proof card | **SUPPORTED THROUGH ACTIVATION** — `FEC-specific guidance helps your team move from ready-built playbooks to daily execution.` |
| Demo page | **A SUPPORT PATH FOR THE WORK AHEAD** — `Understand the implementation steps, team participation, and support available for your facility.` |
| Feature page | **BUILT-IN SYSTEMS. HANDS-ON FEC GUIDANCE.** — `Start with the work already mapped and get support that understands the operating context behind it.` |
| Footer / brand statement | `You bring the brand. We provide the playbook—and the FEC experience to help your team run it.` |

Once approved and provable, replace the general language with operational details such as a named implementation lead, onboarding stages, recurring review cadence, office hours, response target, or support channel. Specifics are more credible than a superlative and reduce booking hesitation.

## Visual Direction From the Design Review

The homepage has the right **Bold Sports/Action** foundation; this new message should reinforce it rather than add more conventional SaaS cards. Use black and white as the structural contrast, cyan only to mark action and proof, large stage numbers, diagonal cuts, and the FEC Playbook™ play-button symbol as a repeatable geometric device. The visual metaphor is not “software screens”; it is a prepared playbook moving from strategy into a team’s daily execution.

The demo page should inherit this system: a left-led headline, a tangible visual artefact such as a “Revenue Review” worksheet, a real playbook map, or an operator handoff diagram, and fewer soft, centered cards. This makes the sales appointment feel like access to a prepared operating system, not a standard SaaS demonstration.

## Recommended Implementation Order

| Priority | Change | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Replace the hero subheadline and helper text; update the hero proof strip. | Makes “pre-built, not blank” clear above the fold. |
| **2** | Add “The Playbook Arrives Built” after the Revenue Cycle. | Explains the delivery model before visitors evaluate screenshots or features. |
| **3** | Update the Revenue Playbooks introduction and footer line. | Prevents visitors from reading the eight cards as generic modules. |
| **4** | Add the support path content and stronger action-brand visual proof to `/book-a-demo`. | Carries the main-site promise through the conversion moment. |
| **5** | Extend the message to Features and How It Works. | Provides message consistency for visitors who explore before booking. |

## Approval Needed Before Build

The first three homepage changes can be implemented immediately using the confirmed offer inventory. To turn “support” into a stronger, proof-led conversion claim, please confirm any specific commitments that are true: implementation owner, onboarding length or phases, support channels, response expectations, recurring calls, office hours, training, or post-launch optimization. If no quantified commitments are approved, the concrete but non-absolute support language above is the safest and most persuasive version.
