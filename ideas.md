### Approach A: Dark Command Center

**Design Movement**: Industrial Digital / Operator Dashboard Aesthetic

**Core Principles**:
1. Authority through darkness — deep charcoal and near-black backgrounds signal serious business software
2. Electric cyan and cobalt blue as power accents that cut through the dark with high contrast
3. Asymmetric, data-driven layouts that feel like a real operator's command center
4. Bold, condensed typography that commands attention without being decorative

**Color Philosophy**: Dark slate (#0D1117) background with FEC Playbook cyan (#00AEEF) and cobalt (#1565C0) as primary accents. White text for maximum contrast. Gradient overlays from dark navy to near-black. The darkness signals "serious platform" while the electric blue signals "modern and energetic."

**Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetric split layouts — left-heavy hero with platform screenshot on right. Diagonal section dividers. Full-width dark sections alternating with slightly lighter card sections. Sticky nav with blur backdrop.

**Signature Elements**:
1. Glowing cyan borders and highlights on key UI elements
2. Platform screenshots in angled device frames with glow effects
3. Gradient text on key headlines (white to cyan)

**Interaction Philosophy**: Smooth scroll reveals, counter animations for stats, hover glow effects on cards, parallax on hero screenshot.

**Animation**: Entrance animations via Framer Motion — staggered fade-up on section content, number counters for stats, subtle floating animation on hero screenshot.

**Typography System**: Montserrat Black/ExtraBold for headlines (matching brand guide), Montserrat Medium for body. Uppercase tracking on section labels.

### Approach B: Bold Sports/Action Brand (CHOSEN)

**Design Movement**: Sports Brand / Action Marketing — think Nike meets SaaS

**Core Principles**:
1. High-contrast black and white as the foundation, with FEC blue as the single power color
2. Oversized, impact-weight typography that treats words as visual design elements
3. Diagonal cuts and angular geometry that convey momentum and forward motion
4. Platform screenshots as hero visual proof, not decorative elements

**Color Philosophy**: True black (#0A0A0A) and white (#FFFFFF) as the base — maximum contrast. FEC Playbook cyan (#00AEEF) and cobalt blue (#1565C0) as the only accent colors, used sparingly for maximum impact. Gradient from cobalt to cyan on key CTAs and highlights. This mirrors the brand guide's black/white/blue palette exactly.

**Layout Paradigm**: Full-bleed sections with angular clip-path transitions between dark and light. Oversized section numbers. Left-aligned text blocks with right-side visual content. Sticky nav that transitions from transparent to solid on scroll.

**Signature Elements**:
1. The FEC Playbook "play button" logo mark used as a recurring geometric motif
2. Platform screenshots in browser-frame mockups with dramatic drop shadows
3. Bold stat callouts (large numbers with small descriptors) scattered throughout

**Interaction Philosophy**: Scroll-triggered animations that feel like a sports broadcast — fast, confident, purposeful. No decorative animations, only functional motion.

**Animation**: Fast fade-up entrances (0.3s), counter animations on stats, smooth hover states on cards. Screenshot reveal with clip-path animation.

**Typography System**: Montserrat Black (900) for H1/H2, Montserrat Bold (700) for H3/subheads, Montserrat Regular for body. All-caps section labels with letter-spacing. Large display numbers for stats.

### Approach C: Neon Gradient / FEC Energy

**Design Movement**: Modern SaaS with FEC/Entertainment Energy

**Core Principles**:
1. Gradient-forward design that captures the energy of a family entertainment center
2. Deep navy-to-black backgrounds with vibrant gradient overlays
3. Card-based content architecture with glass-morphism effects
4. Playful but professional — energetic without being childish

**Color Philosophy**: Deep navy (#050B1A) to black backgrounds. Gradient accents using FEC blue (#1565C0) to cyan (#00AEEF) to electric blue (#0EA5E9). Glass cards with subtle blue tints. This captures the "entertainment" feel while remaining professional enough for B2B SaaS.

**Layout Paradigm**: Centered hero with full-width gradient background. Bento grid for features. Horizontal scrolling testimonial strip. Floating card elements with depth.

**Signature Elements**:
1. Gradient mesh backgrounds on hero and CTA sections
2. Glass-morphism cards with blue-tinted borders
3. Animated gradient borders on key CTA buttons

**Interaction Philosophy**: Smooth, fluid interactions with spring physics. Hover effects that feel alive. Gradient animations that subtly shift.

**Animation**: Gradient background animation, spring-based hover effects, staggered card entrances, smooth scroll-linked parallax.

**Typography System**: Montserrat ExtraBold for headlines, Montserrat Regular for body. Gradient text on key phrases. Clean, modern hierarchy.

## Selected Approach: **Approach B — Bold Sports/Action Brand**

This approach best matches the FEC Playbook brand guide (black, white, blue palette), the Montserrat typography system, and the bold, operator-focused messaging. The angular, high-contrast design signals authority and confidence — exactly what FEC operators need to see from a platform they're trusting to run their business.
